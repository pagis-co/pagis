A Pagis server runs on a VM in a data center, so every Computer on it
reaches the internet from a data-center address. Many sites treat such an
address as a bot. They show CAPTCHAs, refuse sign-ups, block some pages and
mark mail as spam.

The **Home Exit** is one computer of yours that runs the Pagis Client App,
connected to the server. When you choose it, the Computers of your Agents
reach the internet through it, and sites see your own connection. Only a
computer of yours carries your Agents' traffic. The server never sends it
through the computer of another Person.

A Local Installation has no Home Exit. Its Computers run on your own
machine and already reach the internet from your connection.

## Know the cost before you turn it on

When a computer of yours is the Home Exit:

- Sites see the internet address of that computer for every page the
  Agents open.
- A block or an abuse report lands on that address, and sites can link the
  Agents' accounts to the household's own accounts.
- Every byte that a Computer loads crosses that connection twice, once in
  and once out, and its upload speed limits the pages.
- Some internet providers forbid a proxy service in their terms.
- The Agents reach nothing on that computer or on its local network. The
  Client App refuses every address of the home network, also for a name
  that resolves there.

## Choose a computer

A computer can be the Home Exit while the Client App on it is connected to
the server ([Connect to a server](/client-app/connect-to-a-server)). The
Client App then offers the computer to the server as a possible Home Exit.

1. Open **Settings → Hosts** in the Product App. The **Home Exit** card is
   under your Hosts. It shows only on a server.
2. Select the computer in the list. A computer whose Client App does not
   run now shows "(not connected)".
3. Read the costs, then select **Turn on**.

Each awake Computer of yours switches at once, with no restart. The
browser and the shells of the Computer keep their pages and their
windows, but their open connections close, and the next connections go
through the Home Exit. A Computer that sleeps takes the choice when it
wakes. When a Computer does not switch, the card names it and says why.
That Computer keeps its old path and takes the choice at its next wake.

To use another computer, select it in the same list. To stop, select
**Turn off**. Your Computers then reach the internet from the server
again, at once.

## The exit label

An awake Computer that goes through the Home Exit names its exit in use
beside its state on the Desk, and its Agent reads the same line in the
result of each action on the screen:

- `exit: MacBook Pro` says that the connections leave through the computer
  of that name.
- `exit: server` says that you chose a Home Exit, but that computer is not
  connected now, so the connections leave from the server.
- No label says that the Computer reaches the internet from the server,
  because you chose no Home Exit or the Administrator turned it off.

Sites can notice a sudden change of address, so the label changes at once
when the path changes.

## When the computer sleeps

The Home Exit carries traffic only while its Client App runs and is
connected. When the computer sleeps, loses its network or quits the
Client App, each connection that it carried closes. The next connections
of your Computers leave from the server, and the label says `exit: server`.
A Schedule that browses at night keeps working, from the server's address.
When the Client App connects again, the next connections go through it
again.

A laptop that travels takes the address of the network that it joins.

## The tray

While exit traffic flows through a computer, the tray item of its Client
App says so at the top of its menu, such as "Home Exit: 3 connections,
12.4 MB carried". The count is the bytes that the computer carried since
the Client App started. **Turn Off Home Exit** under it turns your Home
Exit off on the server, as **Turn off** in Settings does.

## When the Administrator turns it off

An Administrator can turn the Home Exit off for every Person of the
server. Then every Computer reaches the internet from the server, and the
card says that the Administrator turned it off. Your choice stays. It is in
effect again when the Administrator turns the Home Exit on
([Home Exit](/server/home-exit) for Administrators).

## What does not go through it

- A tool in the terminal that ignores `HTTPS_PROXY` connects from the
  server. `pagis-apt` is such a tool.
- A private address of the server's network, such as one that the
  Administrator opened with `PAGIS_COMPUTER_ALLOW`, leaves from the server.
  An internal name of that network does not resolve at home, so a Computer
  reaches such a destination by its address.
- The live screen of a Computer does not use the Home Exit.
- In Home mode, the browser of a Computer makes WebRTC calls over TCP
  alone, or not at all.
