---
title: Calendar
description: Events, recurrence and meeting links in your Workspace.
---

Open Calendar in the sidebar, or in You on the Mobile App. Choose Day,
Week or Month. Use Previous, Today, Next or the date field to change the
range. Day and Week show an hour grid, with all-day Events above it.
Overlapping Events appear side by side. The Week grid scrolls sideways on
a phone. Month shows three Events in each day cell; choose "+N more" to
open that day. Timed Events display in your device's time zone.

Choose New event. Enter a title, start, end and time zone. For an all-day
Event, the end date is the first day outside the Event. Add a description,
location, attendees and their participation status as needed.

Choose a repeat preset or enter a custom RFC 5545 RRULE. Changes apply to
the complete series. Delete this occurrence removes only the selected
occurrence. Delete series removes the complete Event.

Reminders are saved as minutes before the start, separated by commas.
Reminder delivery as Notifications is not built. An Event can hold an HTTPS
meeting link. Choose Join meeting to open it.

Not built: invitations by mail, free/busy reads, Disclosure Rules, Agent
calendar tools and calendar Grants.
