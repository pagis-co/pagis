Connect to a Pagis server

Your sprites work on your server. On this phone you answer them and give them work.

  1. 1

    On a computer where you use Pagis, open Settings › Sessions.

  2. 2

    Select Sign in another browser or app.

  3. 3

    Scan the QR code with this phone. The code works for 5 minutes.

or

No computer signed in? Ask an administrator for an invite link.
